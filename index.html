Pool Tables in St. Petersburg

Ranked by proximity-to-downtown first, then table quality.

Because downtown St. Pete has bars with pool tables, not real billiard clubs. The order of this list is the honest shape of the problem: walkability buys you atmosphere and a dinner menu; it does not buy you a level 9-footer.

1

The Lure

661 Central Ave · dead center downtown
Closest decent roomSushi / tapas / beer11am–10/11pmBar-grade tables

Closest decent room with tables, dead center downtown. A couple of tables, pool-hall category on Yelp, sushi/tapas/beer, low pressure — the local line is “a couple tables and it’s pretty low pressure.”

Tradeoff: bar-grade tables, and it’s a food-and-drinks venue first.

Sources:YelpReddit · r/StPetersburgFL
2

There Goes The Neighborhood

129 1st Ave N · across from Tequila Daisy
Pro-perfect locationPool · darts · Golden TeeFoodCall ahead re: tables

Pro-perfect downtown location with pool tables, dart boards, a Golden Tee, and food. Locals call it low-key even on weekends.

Quirk: it’s a locals’ bar — a $25/yr membership gets 25% off food and drinks.
Call ahead on the tables. The recent mention was past tense and this spot’s lineup shifts.
Sources:St Pete RisingILOVETHEBURG
3

Enigma Bar & Lounge

1110 Central Ave · EDGE district, ~½ mi north
Open till 3am, 7 days2 tables + darts2-for-1 HH 1–9pmLGBTQ+ friendlyNon-smoking + patio

Two pool tables plus a dart board, and it’s the only downtown-corridor option open until 3am, seven days, with 2-for-1 happy hour 1–9pm. LGBTQ+ friendly lounge, DJs, drag shows, non-smoking interior plus patio.

Pick this for late night.

Sources:YelpEDGE District

Best pool value50¢ a gameFree WednesdaysSunday tournaments 7:30pmLeague table for rent

The honest answer for best pool value near downtown: 50¢ a game, free on Wednesdays, tournaments every Sunday at 7:30pm, and a league table you can rent. A 55-year-old dive with a serious regulars’ scene (reportedly a Kerouac haunt).

No gloss, all felt.

Two caveats

Several aggregator lists here (Atly / Yelp / ChalkySticks) are auto-generated and mix in Clearwater, Largo, and Tampa spots — The Corner Pocket and Crooked Cue are not St. Pete, and Brewlands is Tampa.

And anything described as a “pool table” in a restaurant is usually a 7-footer, which matters if you’re a serious shooter.
